# M8b — Reading — Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Players read the library M8a writes. Each new quest day gets one published article the player hasn't had,
mostly from their goals and class. Today's reading card opens the reader (board D3). A one-sentence takeaway claims the
quest's 10 Wisdom XP and counts towards "articles read". The Chronicle lists the past readings (board D5). A Mage from
level 5 uses Insight to choose tomorrow's topic from 3. The phone keeps today's and tomorrow's articles, so a day built
offline still has its reading.

**Architecture:** The server picks the article when it builds a day, in the same pure planning step as the quests
(`_shared/reading.ts`, called by `planAdvance` and by onboarding's day 1). It stores the pick on `quest_days.article_id`,
the column that has been there since 0002. The state that `lmu-sync` returns names today's article, tomorrow's pick, the
last 30 readings and Insight's options. Each response also carries the full text of the articles the phone doesn't
hold yet: the request lists the ones it has (`have`) and up to 3 it asks for (`want`). The phone keeps them in a new
SQLite table. Migration 0015 does the following:
- adds the foreign key and an index to `quest_days.article_id`;
- adds `character_state.insight`;
- lets `insert_day` keep only published article ids;
- makes `complete_quest` refuse the reading quest on a day without an article, and makes complete/undo count
  `articles_read`;
- adds `set_insight` for the new `insight` event;
- drops `reserve_ai_call`, unused since M8a.

**Tech Stack:** As M8a on the server (Deno Edge Functions, `npm:postgres@3.4.9`, Vitest, pgTAP through `db:test`). As
M7d on the phone (Expo SDK 57, React Native 0.86.3, expo-router, zustand 5, expo-sqlite, Jest 29 + RNTL 14). No new
dependency and no native module, so the M7d development build (998b72c0) runs it through Metro. No Claude call, no
monthly cost.

**Specs:**
- MVP spec §1.3: Chronicle's past articles.
- MVP spec §1.4: the article card's loading, empty and offline states.
- MVP spec §2.5: Mage, Insight at level 5.
- MVP spec §4.1 "Assignment":
  - one unread published article per new day, about 60 % from the player's goals and class;
  - stored on the day and never regenerated;
  - the phone caches articles for offline days;
  - Insight;
  - the article quest is Wisdom minor 10 XP and counts for the streak;
  - evergreen articles fill any gap.
- MVP spec §5: each day has exactly 1 core + 2 side + 1 article; only checked articles are shown.
- Architecture §3: `articles` and `article_sources` are readable by players when published.
- Architecture §4.1: `sync` returns "a few cached articles".
- Architecture §6: the SQLite cache keeps articles for offline days.
- Visual spec §4.1:
  - D1, Today's reading card: "6 min · Wisdom +10 XP · one takeaway", READ;
  - D3, Today's reading: sources section, a one-sentence takeaway claims +10 XP;
  - D5, past readings.
- Roadmap row 8b, and the rows carried to M8b:
  - M6d: Insight shows "Unlocked · it arrives in a later update" until its screen exists;
  - M8a: remove `reserve_ai_call` in the next migration that touches the AI log.

## Plain summary

When M8b is done:

- **Every new day brings a reading.** When the server builds a day it picks one published article you haven't had.
  - **Where it comes from:** on three days in five it comes from your areas. Each goal and your class point to one of
    the 8 topics; for example, the Body goal points to Fitness and movement and a Mage to Learning. On the other two
    days it comes from the other topics, so you meet everything.
  - **Order:** the oldest published article comes first, and two days in a row don't share a topic when another is
    waiting.
  - **When nothing is new:** if you've read everything, an evergreen article comes back, the one you read longest ago.
  - **Before any article is published:** the reading quest waits, as it does today.
- **Today's reading card** shows the article's title, "6 min · Wisdom +10 XP · one takeaway" and READ.
- **The reader** follows the approved board D3:
  - the topic, the title and the summary;
  - the sections, each with its source numbers and the occasional System note;
  - the takeaways and the reflection question;
  - "Your takeaway, one sentence" with CLAIM +10 XP;
  - the sources, each opening in the browser.
  - After the claim you get the usual "Quest cleared" with "Read again", and no rating, because an article has no rung
    to move.
- **"Articles read" now counts.** First Page, Lamplighter and Archivist can be earned. The server refuses a reading
  claim on a day that has no article.
- **Past readings in the Chronicle:** your last 30 readings, newest first, each with its date. Opening one shows the
  article again with the takeaway you wrote. A reading that isn't on the phone comes with the next sync.
- **Insight (Mage, level 5).** Under today's reading, choose tomorrow's topic from 3: topics with an article you haven't
  read, the ones you've read least recently first. You can change the choice until the day ends. It applies to the
  next day the server builds and then lapses. The class path now shows Insight as unlocked.
- **Offline days:** each sync also sends tomorrow's article, so a day the phone builds offline has its reading. The
  server keeps it.
- **Your yes is needed for the deploy:** migration 0015 and the functions. It adds no monthly cost and changes nothing
  of the wedding's.
- **Players see real articles only after you publish some** (`npm run articles -- publish …`, M8a Task 14). Until then
  the reading quest waits, as it does today. A development screen shows the reader with a sample article, so the
  emulator check doesn't have to wait.

## Decisions this plan makes

These are Claude's recommendations, under the owner's "use your recommendations" for this session's build loop. The
library, the assignment rule and Insight were approved with the MVP spec (2026-10-10); these decisions fill in its
details. The deploy still waits for the owner's yes in chat.

1. **The server picks the article when it builds a day, and never changes it.**
   - **The chooser** is pure: `chooseArticle` in `_shared/reading.ts`. `planAdvance` uses it for every new day (a kept
     offline day included), and onboarding's `startArgs` uses it for day 1.
   - **The order it tries:**
     - Insight's topic, if the player chose one on the previous day;
     - the day's group: the player's areas on days 1, 2, 3, 6, 7, 8 … (3 in 5, the spec's "about 60 %"), the other
       topics on days 4, 5, 9, 10 …;
     - the other group.
   - **Within a group:** the oldest published unread article first. An article from the same topic as the last one the
     player got goes after the others.
   - **When nothing is unread,** the day gets the published evergreen article given to this player longest ago (spec:
     "evergreen fills any gap"). With no published evergreen article, the day gets none.
   - **No article at all:** the day's `article_id` stays null and the reading quest waits, as before M8b. The same
     holds on a day built without quests (`no_quests`).
   - **What "unread" means:** never given to this player, the definition M8a's `article_short_players` already uses
     for the night's demand.
2. **A player's areas** are one topic per goal and one for the class, in the content package (`GOAL_CLUSTERS`,
   `CLASS_CLUSTERS`):

   | Goal or class | Topic |
   |---|---|
   | body | movement |
   | energy | sleep |
   | learn | learning |
   | focus | focus |
   | routine | habits |
   | warrior | movement |
   | mage | learning |
   | rogue | psychology |
   | monk | philosophy |

   So a player has 1–3 areas. History and the rest come on the other days.
3. **What the phone gets.**
   - **The state** gains:
     - `today.article` (id, topic, title, minutes) or null;
     - `nextArticle`: what tomorrow's day would get, as of now;
     - `readings`: the last 30 days that had an article, newest first, each with its date, title, topic, minutes and
       the player's takeaway, or null;
     - `insight`: for a Mage from level 5 on an open day, the options and the choice; otherwise null.
   - **The full articles go beside the state,** in the response's `articles` (`ArticleBody`: the text, the takeaways,
     the question and the sources renumbered 1…n with their hosts). They aren't stored in the cached state.
   - **What a response carries:** today's and tomorrow's articles unless the request's `have` lists them, plus up to 3
     the phone asks for in `want` (a past reading). `have` holds at most 64 ids.
   - **On the phone:** a new SQLite table `articles` keeps today's, tomorrow's and the readings' articles, and drops
     the rest after each sync. That is at most 32 articles, about 300 KB.
   - **Cost on the wire:** about 8 KB a day once, rather than every sync.
4. **The offline day** (M6e builds one at a time) takes `nextArticle`, and the `provisional_day` event carries its id
   (`articleId`, optional for older phones). The server keeps the phone's article when it is still published;
   otherwise it chooses as usual.
5. **The reader** is the article quest's own screen (`Quest.tsx` for the article slot). It follows board D3:
   - the label, "6 MIN · WISDOM +10 XP", the topic, the title, the summary;
   - the sections: a heading, the paragraphs, the source numbers ("[1] [2]"), and a System note in the System's box;
   - the takeaways, numbered, then the reflection question;
   - "Your takeaway, one sentence" with "Claim +10 XP": the existing sentence check, 1–280 characters, no control
     characters;
   - the sources: "1. Title · host", each opening in the browser.

   After the claim the screen shows the usual "Quest cleared", without the rating (no ladder) and with "Read again".
   When the article isn't on the phone yet, the screen says so and asks the next sync for it. When the day has no
   article, it shows the existing line ("Today's reading opens once the daily articles arrive…") and no claim.
6. **The server's rules for the claim.**
   - `complete_quest` refuses the article slot on a day whose `article_id` is null (`lmu:no_article`, with a notice on
     the phone).
   - `articles_read` goes up with each claimed reading, an evergreen re-read included, and down with its undo. So the
     three reading achievements can finally be earned.
   - The XP is unchanged: Wisdom minor, 10.
7. **Past readings.**
   - The Chronicle gets a "Past readings" section under the growth card: date and title, newest first. Today's reading
     is left out, since it's on Today.
   - Opening one goes to `/reading/<id>`, which shows the reader with "Your takeaway" (or "No takeaway was written that
     day") and no claim.
   - An article the phone doesn't hold is asked for (`want`) and arrives with the next sync. Offline, the screen says
     it comes with the next sync.
8. **Insight** (spec §2.5: Mage, level 5, "choose tomorrow's article topic from 3").
   - **Where it shows:** on Today, under the reading card, for a Mage from level 5 on a day the server built. It isn't
     shown on an offline day.
   - **The options:** up to 3 topics that hold an article the player hasn't had, other than today's. The topics read
     least recently come first; ties rotate by day so the same three don't always show.
   - **Choosing:** sends an `insight` event; the choice shows at once and can be changed until the day ends (the last
     one wins). The server checks it against a fresh snapshot:
     - the day is open, otherwise `day_closed`;
     - the player is a Mage from level 5, otherwise `ability_not_ready`;
     - the topic is one of the 8, otherwise `bad_change`;
     - the topic still has an unread article, otherwise `topic_empty`.
     `levelmeup.set_insight` then stores `{cluster, day}` on `character_state.insight`. It is idempotent per event id,
     like every write, and bumps the version, so a day being built at that moment plans again.
   - **When it applies:** to the first day built after the day it was chosen on. If the player was away, that is still
     the next day built. It then lapses; a later day ignores it.
   - **Elsewhere:** it isn't an adjustment of today (`levelmeup.adjust` stays as 0013 left it), and it isn't an ability
     with a cooldown or an achievement. On the class path it reads "Unlocked", and `ABILITIES_LATER` becomes empty.
9. **Migration 0015** (`0015_reading.sql`):
   - **`quest_days.article_id`:**
     - a foreign key to `articles` (`on delete set null`; every existing value is null);
     - an index `(user_id, article_id) where article_id is not null`, for the per-player "unread" reads and M8a's
       demand check.
   - **`character_state.insight`:** jsonb, null by default.
   - **`insert_day`:** keeps an article id only when that article is published, otherwise stores null. That defends
     the shared library; the server only picks published ones anyway.
   - **`complete_quest` and `undo_quest`:** redefined from 0004 with the article rules of decision 6.
   - **`set_insight`:** a new security-definer function, executable by `lmu_service` only.
   - **`reserve_ai_call`:** dropped (carried row). Its DB tests move to `reserve_ai_spend`.
10. **Characters outside the app fonts' Latin subset** (an article may name "Erdős" or use "≈") render in the phone's
    own font: Android falls back character by character. The development reading screen's sample article contains
    both, so the emulator check sees it. No article text is changed or refused for this.
11. **Not in M8b:**
    - search;
    - bookmarks;
    - a text-size control (the system font scale applies);
    - sharing;
    - rating an article;
    - readings older than the last 30;
    - images;
    - reading progress or a reading timer: the claim is honour-system with a takeaway, as for every quest.

  - `_shared/advance.ts:236` and `:276`;

- **Where the article already shows or counts:**
  - `Quest.tsx:175` shows `PLAY_TEXT.quest.article` instead of a claim panel.
  - `STREAK_SLOTS` includes the article.
  - `character_state.articles_read` exists (0002) but nothing changes it.
  - The achievements `article-1`, `article-10` and `article-30` read `articlesRead`.
- **What 0014 left:**
  - `articles` (sections stored camelCase: `heading`, `paragraphs`, `systemNote`, `sourceIds`) and `article_sources`
    hold only the sources an article cites, with `idx` the brief's ids, so they may be sparse.
  - `lmu_player` may select published articles and their sources.
  - `quest_days.article_id` has no foreign key and no index.
- **`levelmeup.complete_quest` and `undo_quest` were last defined in 0004.** `insert_day` was defined in 0003; it isn't
  a security definer and is called by `start_player`, `restart_player` and `advance`.
- **The sync response type flows to the phone:** `ServerState = ReturnType<typeof stateView>`. Fixtures build states
  with the server's own `stateView` (`apps/mobile/test/support/fixtures.ts`).
- **The phone's SQLite migrations** are a list in `src/data/local.ts`, `pragma user_version`. The last of the 7 entries
  clears the cached state for the new shape.
- **The app's fonts are Latin subsets** (`src/theme/glyphs.ts`); `glyphs.test.ts` covers the content package's strings
  only.
- **The spike article** (`article-<n>`, habits, M8a Task 13) is in `review`. Nothing is published yet.
