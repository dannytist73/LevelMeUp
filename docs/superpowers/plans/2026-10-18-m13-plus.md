# M13: LevelMeUp Plus (planned, not built)

**In plain words.** How LevelMeUp earns money, from the owner's choices of 2026-10-18 on the research report
(`docs/research/2026-10-18-monetization-onboarding.md` §5): "I do want the 7-day free trial, but after that let's just go
with your recommendations." So:

- **A generous free game forever, plus "LevelMeUp Plus"**, sold monthly, yearly or once (Lifetime). No ads.
- **Prices:** US $4.99 a month, $29.99 a year, $69.99 Lifetime; Philippines ₱129, ₱699, ₱1,490 (set by hand in Play
  Console). Review after three months of data.
- **A 7-day free trial on the yearly plan only**, shown with its timeline ("Today: Plus unlocked. Day 5: we remind you.
  Day 7: ₱699 is charged unless you cancel"), our own day-5 reminder, and a "Start Day 1 free" button as big as the buy
  button.
- **Where the offer appears:** once at the end of the onboarding (after the reveal, now that saving moved to day 1), once
  on the Day 7 recap, then only when the player taps something that is Plus. Never during a slip, a hard day, a rest or
  the pain route.
- **RevenueCat** handles Google's receipts, renewals and refunds (free under $2,500 a month of revenue). Our server stays
  the one answer to "is this player Plus?", refreshed by pulling (no webhook, so every function still checks a sign-in).
- **Free forever:** every quest, ladder, class, stat, streak and ability; safety, the pain route and rest days; the Shadow
  pact; reminders, saving and deleting; every article and Story Deck, the archive included.
- **Beta testers:** no codes. The Founder title for everyone in the closed test; Plus for life after 14 days of it, granted
  from our own server to their saved Google account.

## What Plus sells on day one (the owner chose "Themes, charts, frames", 2026-10-18)

Nothing that Plus would sell exists yet: the research's Plus is cosmetics (outfits, Shadow skins, themes, title frames),
the full history with charts, more custom quests (custom quests aren't built), and later AI extras. A paywall with
nothing behind it would break Google's rules and the owner's own "never pretend" rule. **The first Plus set:**

1. **Themes:** the two other palettes from the proposals canvas, System Blue and Mint Night (Moonlight stays free). Cheap:
   every colour is already a token; the work is reading the palette at run time instead of at load.
2. **The full Chronicle:** today's calendar stays free as it is; Plus adds stat charts over all time. (Nothing free is
   taken away.)
3. **Title frames for the portrait,** and the Founder frame for beta testers (never sold).

Later, when built: custom quests (1 free, 5 with Plus), alternate class outfits (new art, a cost to propose first), and
the AI extras with a fair-use cap.

## The owner's steps (calendar time; nothing here can be done for you)

These take days to weeks, so they can start now, in this order:

1. **Play Console, the account:** check Settings → Developer account → Account details (the path the research found; not
   checked on your account). A *personal* account shows your
   full address on the store page once you sell; a business address or an organization account (a D-U-N-S number)
   avoids that.
2. **A Philippine accountant**, before the first sale: BIR registration and VAT or percentage tax. Google collects VAT
   itself only for developers outside the Philippines.
3. **Play Console, the app:** create LevelMeUp (package `com.dannytist.levelmeup`), the payments profile with your TIN,
   and enrol in the 15% service-fee tier *before* the first sale.
4. **The closed test:** a new personal account needs 12 or more testers opted in for 14 days in a row before production.
   The beta becomes that test; testers install from the Play Store link (the sideloaded APK doesn't count). Aim for 15 to
   20. Before moving testers over, decide the signing key with me (a guest who must uninstall loses the character).
5. **RevenueCat:** a free account and project, and Google service credentials connected (they can take up to 36 hours to
   work).
6. **The products** (once the app is on Play): `plus_monthly` and `plus_yearly` subscriptions (the yearly base plan with
   a 7-day free-trial offer for new subscribers) and `plus_lifetime`, a one-time product; PH prices by hand. License
   testers for trying the purchase without paying.

## What I build

1. **The entitlement, server first:** a `levelmeup.entitlements` table (RLS and its pgTAP test), one row per grant
   (`plus`, source `beta_founder` / `play`, an end date or none). The state view says whether the player is Plus; the app
   never decides alone. An owner command grants `beta_founder` by Google email, at the owner's yes each time.
2. **RevenueCat in the app** (a new native module and a new build; `react-native-purchases`, the owner's yes to download):
   `logIn` with the player's id, the offerings, purchase, restore. After a purchase the app calls an `lmu-` function that
   asks RevenueCat and writes the row; a daily `lmu-` cron refreshes every Plus row (renewals, refunds, expiry).
3. **The offer screen** (no dark patterns, per the research §3): what Plus adds and what stays free, the three plans with
   the yearly price shown per year, the trial's timeline, two buttons of equal size, Restore, Terms and Privacy. After the
   reveal; once at Day 7; then only from a Plus item.
4. **Settings → LevelMeUp Plus:** the status, Restore, and Manage subscription (Google requires it).
5. **The trial's day-5 reminder** (a local note, if notifications are on) and an in-app card on day 5.
6. **The Plus set** the owner picks, each item behind the entitlement.
7. **Privacy:** the in-app privacy page and the Data safety form list purchase history; deleting a character also deletes
   its RevenueCat customer.

Costs: RevenueCat $0 below $2,500 a month of revenue, then 1%. Google keeps 15%.
