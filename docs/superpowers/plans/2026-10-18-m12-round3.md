# M12: Round 3 (UI/UX Pro Max), sounds and live timers

**In plain words.** The owner approved the Round 3 redesign on the proposals canvas
(https://claude.ai/artifact/Tfm6zj7rAxH6wFyEsqDtBF, page "Round 3 · UI/UX Pro Max", 2026-10-18: "build the
recommendations that you have"), and asked for sounds and timers in the notification shade ("we want things to be
interactive, with visual and sound interactions on the relevant parts"). The audit behind Round 3 is
`docs/2026-10-18-ux-audit.md` (25 findings). What changes for a player:

1. **Today says what to do next.** A one-row header, one notice strip, an "Up next" card holding the only bright
   button, and everything else as quiet rows. The day closes once the core quest and the reading are done; side
   quests become "If you want more". Clearing a quest leads straight on to the next thing.
2. **A quest's log comes first**, with its button fixed at the bottom of the screen; How, Why and Safety fold below.
3. **A Library tab** (the fourth tab): today's reading, the lessons you kept, past readings.
4. **A shorter first run**: three chapters with a real progress bar and Back on every step. The voice, the equipment,
   the slip question, the vow, the Pact and saving with Google move to small one-time cards on days 1 to 3.
5. **Every tap answers** (a ripple), the tab you're on is obvious, edges you can see, real loading, empty and offline
   states, one back bar everywhere, text that can grow to 200%.
6. **Sounds** for the moments that matter (a quest claimed, XP, a stat rank, the streak, a level, a rest or timer
   over, a card turned, a lesson kept, the Shadow), made on this PC (no licence, no download), with a Sounds switch.
7. **Live timers in the notification shade**: a running quest timer, a rest between sets and the Trial of Stillness
   show a live countdown on the lock screen and in the shade, and an alert when time is up.

Branch `m12-round3`, stacked on `m11-lantern-deck` (`d44c54a`; M11 isn't merged yet). Built inline (the owner's way:
plan, build, then test and verify; no Superpowers skills). App version stays 0.6.0 / versionCode 7: no build has
shipped M11 yet, so M11 and M12 reach phones together.

## Decisions

1. **Four tabs: Today, Library, Character, Chronicle** (board R3-5). The Codex leaves the bottom of Character and the
   past readings leave Chronicle; both live in Library. A kept lesson opens its reading's cards.
2. **When a day is done** (boards R3-1, R3-4): the core quest (and a recovery quest, while one is open) plus today's
   reading, when the day has one. Side quests are optional extras. This is a display rule on the phone (Today and the
   widget): the streak rule is the server's and doesn't change (the core quest, or any two quests, spec §2.4), so no
   server change and no deploy.
3. **"Up next"**, in order: an open recovery quest, the core quest, the reading, then side quests (today's
   `nextQuest`, with the core quest first while it's open). It is the only card with an accent button. Hold-to-clear
   goes: a check-off quest is cleared by its row's check circle, with "Undo" on a snackbar for 5 seconds (the
   existing `undo` event), because a single tap can be a brush.
4. **Notices fold into one strip**: the training notice, check-in notes, the update banner and the queue of System
   notices show one at a time in a single dismissible strip above Up next, with "1 of 3".
5. **The quest screen's button is a bar fixed at the bottom** ("Log 2 sets · +10 XP"). The log panels keep their
   own logic; a small context lets the screen draw the panel's claim button in the bar (no provider, as in the deck
   and tests: the button stays inline).
6. **Cleared leads on**: "Next: today's reading · 3 min" (or the next quest) is the primary button, "Back to Today"
   secondary. The rating starts with none selected (the server already treats no rating as Right).
7. **Onboarding in three chapters** (board R3-7): *You* (name, goals), *Your body* (training and limits, then the
   safety check as its own step), *Your day* (reading and focus when asked, then the rhythm), then the analysis and
   the reveal. A true progress bar (steps done of steps shown), Back on every step, Continue fixed at the bottom with
   what's left ("5 of 7 left"). The onboarding sends the System voice, bodyweight training and no pact; the cards on
   days 1 to 3 change them through the events that already exist (`settings`, `training`, the Pact screen).
8. **Set-up cards, days 1 to 3** (one at a time, in the notice strip, never above an open core quest's Up next):
   Day 1 after the first cleared quest: *Save your progress* (guests only) and *Choose your guide's voice*; Day 2:
   *Your equipment* (when strength work is possible) and *When you slip* (with the Pact for "real stakes"); Day 3:
   *Your vow*. Each is offered once and stays in Settings. The monetization research's two optional asks join them:
   "Why now?" fills the vow's words, "What gets in the way?" sits with the slip question (M13 decision; both optional).
9. **The reminder offer** (audit 17): once, on the first day done: "A nudge tomorrow? One reminder at 7:30 AM"
   (half an hour after the player's wake time), Remind me / Not now. Remind me asks Android's permission and turns on
   the morning briefing (M7d).
10. **Interactive outlines** use a new `lineStrong #6A73A0` (3.75:1 on night, 3.22:1 on panel; WCAG 1.4.11). The
    quiet `line` stays for decoration. Touch targets 48 dp. Body, labels and small text may grow to 2× (titles 1.5×).
11. **Sounds** (owner's request): short tones synthesised on this PC (Python + NumPy, 44.1 kHz WAV, each under 1 s),
    played with `expo-audio` (**needs the owner's yes to download, 1.3 MB**). One file imports it
    (`src/ui/sound.ts`, like `haptics.ts`). They mix with the player's music (never pause it), follow the phone's
    media volume, and a Sounds switch sits beside Haptics in Settings → Game (on by default). A sound is never the only
    signal: every one has a visual and most a haptic. Taps on ordinary buttons stay silent (only moments sound).
12. **Live timers**: a local Expo module (`modules/lmu-live-timer`, Kotlin, no new library) posts an ongoing,
    silent notification with Android's own countdown chronometer (it counts down with the app closed and draws
    nothing itself), and removes it at the end. When time is up, the existing timer alert (M7d) fires with a sound;
    a rest gets the same alert. Only when notifications are allowed: the first timer started without permission
    offers it once, in context. A new native module means a new build (the owner's yes).

## Phases (step → how it's verified)

1. **Kit** → `ui.test`, `tokens.test`: `Tap` (ripple + pressed state) under Button, cards, rows, tabs, steppers; the
   tab bar with four tabs and a filled active pill; `lineStrong`; `space.touch 48`; font caps; `TopBar` (48 dp Back,
   the title as a header); `Skeleton`, `StateNote` (empty, offline, failed); `Snackbar` (Undo); reduced motion that
   follows the setting live.
2. **Today** → `today.test`, `game/today.test`: header, notice strip, Up next, Then, Done; day done and its panel; the
   reminder offer; the widget's "Day cleared" follows decision 2.
3. **Quest and Cleared** → `quest.test`, `cleared.test`: log first, the bar, folds; Next on Cleared; no rating
   pre-selected; the deck keeps its draft when closed; keyboard avoidance on Your turn.
4. **Library** → `library.test`: the tab; today's reading, kept lessons that open their cards, past readings; "Take
   out" with Undo.
5. **Character** → `character.test`: character and stats first; achievements with progress; the vow.
6. **Onboarding and set-up cards** → `flow.test`, `onboarding.test`, `setup.test`: chapters, progress, Back, the
   sticky Continue with what's left; the defaults sent; each card once.
7. **Smaller fixes** (audit 19–25) → their screens' tests: the Shadow scene's one primary, plain-word errors, Codex
   undo, sentence-case days.
8. **Sounds** → `sound.test`: the cue table, the switch, mixing set once, a missing module stays quiet.
9. **Live timers** → `liveTimer.test` (JS side, the module mocked); the Kotlin is compiled by the next build and
   checked on the emulator (checklist rows).
10. **Review and record** → a review subagent over the diff; typecheck, all tests, `check:bundle`, `check:portable`;
    checklist rows for the emulator; this file's "As built".

## As built (2026-10-18)

- **Phases 1–7 built and tested** (commits `71a5411` to `bae869c`), then live timers (`126d9b1`) and the sound files
  (`7178932`). App tests 1,030 in 68 suites, content tests 1,113, `tsc` clean, `check:portable` and `check:bundle` clean.
  Nothing is checked on a phone yet: the emulator rows are in `docs/device-verification-checklist.md` (M12, rows 1–23).
- **Decision 2** needed no server change: "Day N cleared" was already a phone-only rule (`game/today.ts`); the widget
  follows it through a new `dayDone` in its snapshot (an older snapshot falls back to "every quest").
- **Decision 4:** the update banner and the check-in notes stay where they were (above the strip): they aren't
  dismissible notices. The check-in's buttons are now quiet (audit 1).
- **Decision 7:** the onboarding sends the System voice, bodyweight training and no pact. The Voice, Gear, Slip, Vow,
  Save and onboarding-Pact screens and routes are gone; the Vow is `/vow` and the slip question `/slip`, opened from the
  cards. The Pact screen's `draft` mode is now used only by the developer Shadow bench (left as it was).
- **Decision 8:** a character is enrolled for the cards when its reveal is accepted (the `setup` offer); a Google-restored
  character never is. The save card opens Settings, where the Google save and its error messages already live.
- **Decision 9:** the reminder offers the morning briefing at its usual time (7:30 AM unless changed), not "half an hour
  after you wake": the wake time isn't kept after the onboarding.
- **Decision 11 (sounds):** the 14 tones are in `assets/sounds` (about 1 MB; `scripts/make-sounds.py` makes them),
  played through expo-audio 57.0.5 (downloaded at the owner's yes; its config plugin set to no microphone and no
  background playback, so it adds no permission we don't use). `playsInSilentMode: false` keeps them quiet on silent or
  vibrate; `mixWithOthers` never pauses the player's music. The moments: the XP stamp (claim), a rank, the streak, a
  level, an achievement, a vow or pact sworn, a timer started and reaching its time, a rest over, a card turned, a
  lesson kept, the check circle and Undo, the day done, the Shadow's stamp. A Sounds switch sits beside Haptics in
  Settings.
- **The review** (a subagent over the whole diff) found, and these were fixed: a check-off core quest was cleared by
  Begin instead of opened; the set-up cards were lost if the app closed on the reveal (now enrolled when the character is
  made); a protected day (a rest, a rest after pain) still pushed Up next (now nothing is asked of it, and an exercise
  resting after pain never holds a day open); a double tap on a check circle sent two completions; an open day with no
  quests said it had ended; the claim bar's space was fixed at 132 dp (now measured, for 2x text); Library's cleared
  reading read "cleared Read again"; a kept lesson older than the phone's 30 readings said it was gone (now asked for);
  "A nudge tomorrow?" named the wrong reminder; the "kind nudge" suggestion promised a reminder nothing turned on (now
  it offers Notifications); the hidden-achievement count included the ones shown; the voice card showed to a player who
  had chosen another voice.
- **Decision 12 (live timers):** quest timers and the rest between sets. The rest has no "time's up" alert of its own
  (only its live countdown, which goes at zero, and the in-app click); quest timers keep M7d's alert. The Trial of
  Stillness has no live countdown yet.
