# The base app: Train, Read, Eat (2026-10-28)

The owner's ask: drop the game and keep a simple, useful app built on three things that help a person build a routine:
a workout tracker on the Kaizen builder, a library of story books, and an AI food log. Grumble Camp is shelved, not
deleted: tag `grumble-camp-0.14.1` (branch `simpler-day`, APK `dist/grumble-camp-0.14.1-preview.apk`). This work is on
branch `base-app`, started from that tag.

Screens for approval: canvas https://claude.ai/artifact/3o5hTJGzeyCmXCbJmLqvWu

**Status (2026-10-28, ralph loop, `docs/2026-10-28-ralph-loop.md`):** the owner picked **Graphite**. B1 (base app and
Train) is built **local-first** on the phone, with Read (5 bundled books) and Eat (manual log) too; APKs 0.15.0 built and

## Decisions (the owner took my four picks, 2026-10-28)

1. **Name:** "LevelMeUp" as the working name; the real name later. Same package (`com.dannytist.levelmeup`), so it
   installs over Grumble Camp.
2. **Plan lengths:** 6 weeks (one Kaizen block), 12 weeks (two blocks), Custom (4 to 52 weeks, or an end date such
   as before a trip) or No end date (keeps going in 6-week rounds). Every 6th week is the easy deload week whatever
   the length. (2026-10-28: the owner found "Freeform" too vague; Custom and No end date replace it.)
3. **Free vs Plus:** Train and Read are free. Eat is free for typing in a food and its numbers by hand; Plus adds AI
   logging from a photo, a sentence or your voice.
4. **Testers' data:** keep their workout history and reading progress; XP, levels and the rest are no longer shown.

## What the app is

Four tabs: **Today**, **Train**, **Read**, **Eat**. "Me" (account, units, reminders, Plus) opens from Today's header.

- **Today:** this week's next workout, the chapter you're on, and today's food against your targets. One tap into each.
- **Train:** your plan (week N of the block, this week's sessions), the workout log, history and each exercise's best.
- **Read:** your current book, downloaded books, more books to download, and a "more coming" shelf when you've read
  them all.
- **Eat:** today's meals and totals (calories and protein first; carbs and fat beside them), and one box to log food.

No XP, levels, classes, Shadow, Grumbles, quests, achievements, shields or camp. No streak. Today and Train show plain
facts ("2 of 3 workouts this week"). Missed days are handled the Kaizen way: the plan waits for you (the next workout
is the one you missed), and after 14 days away it restarts the block from the light week.

## Onboarding: the season setup

Short, one question a screen, every answer changeable later in Train:

1. What the app does (Train, Read, Eat), one screen.
2. Which workout builder: Men's or Women's.
3. Where you are now: new to lifting / coming back after a break / training regularly.
4. **Your plan** (mocked up): length (6 weeks, 12 weeks, Custom with a weeks stepper and its end date, No end date),
   days a week (2, 3, 4 or 5; 5 is marked "for experienced lifters", as the builder says), where you train (Gym,
   Home, Both). Units (kg or lb) default from the phone's region and live in Me.
5. Home equipment, when Home or Both: bodyweight, bands, dumbbells, bench, pull-up bar.
6. The safety questions we already have (pain and health checks); a "yes" keeps the plan gentle and offers rest.
7. Your plan, summed up: week 1 is lighter (2 sets of everything, stop 2–3 reps before failure), weeks 2–5 build up,
   week 6 is the deload (2 sets, half the reps, same weight), then it repeats. First workout and its day.
8. Pick your first book.

Eat is set up the first time you open it (goal and body weight give a calorie and protein target you can change).

## Train: what we keep and what is new

The research behind all of this is `docs/research/kaizen-training.md` (§3 the builders, §3.6 the progression rules).

**Keep (works today):**
- The exercise directory: 53 exercises with steps, cues and pictures, in 15 swap groups
  (`packages/content/src/training/moves.ts`).
- The plan engine in `packages/rules/src/workout.ts`: picks an exercise per slot from what you own, sets each week by
  Kaizen's ramp (2, 3, 3, 4, 4 for the priority slots; 2, 3, 3, 3, 3 for the rest), the week-6 deload, and the week
  moving on by workouts done, not by the calendar.
- The rest timer with its notification-shade countdown, and "last time" numbers prefilling the log.

**New:**
- **Weight on every set.** Today only reps are kept. Each set gets weight (kg or lb), reps (or seconds) and a done tick.
- **The Kaizen rep targets and progression rules** (§3.6): fixed targets for the main lifts (6, 8 or 10 reps),
  double progression for accessories, and the band, assisted and bodyweight rules. After a workout the app suggests
  next week's weight ("all sets hit 12: try 2.5 kg more"); you can always change it.
- **4 and 5 days a week.** Today the plan does 2 or 3; the builder's 4x and 5x splits are in §3.4.
- **Gym or Home, per workout.** A switch at the top of the log. Each exercise is tagged by the equipment it needs, so
  the app knows what works at home. Switching re-picks each exercise for that place, in the same slot.
- **Swap one exercise** (mocked up): the same slot's exercises that fit where you are, each with its last numbers.
  "Just today" or "Keep it for this block". Every exercise keeps its own history, so going back and forth between gym
  and home loses nothing.
- **More gym exercises:** the Kaizen directory's barbell, Smith, machine and cable picks, so shoulders, arms, abs and
  calves have gym options too. Rest by exercise (2–3 min compounds, 1–2 min isolation) instead of one 120 s.
- **History and bests:** each workout, each exercise's sets over time, and its best set.
- **Any day, any order (2026-10-29, the owner's ask):** there are no fixed weekdays; Start opens the next workout
  whenever you train. In Train, tap any workout not done this week and Start opens that one instead; the week ends
  after its last one. Changing days a week keeps the workouts already done that week (a week they already fill moves
  on). Starting a second workout on the same day asks first ("You already trained today", rest helps); it never blocks.
- **Pain** stays one tap away on every exercise and always offers rest, a gentler swap or stopping.
- **Works without signal.** Gyms often have none: workouts are saved on the phone first and sent when online.

## Read: books

- A **book** is one big idea in 6–10 chapters; each chapter is one of our card stories, and one person from the cast
  carries the whole book.
- **Download** a book for offline reading: chapter text and pictures saved on the phone.
- A gentle daily nudge ("Chapter 3 is waiting") in the morning or evening reminder, never a streak.
- **First books** come from stories we already have, grouped by subject and put in a sensible order. Then the 9 AM
  studio writes new books chapter by chapter: it gets the book's outline and the earlier chapters, and keeps the same
  person.
- **Pictures (2026-10-28, owner: the clay and monster pictures don't fit Graphite):** five styles were tried on the same
  three covers (graphite pencil, flat gouache, ink line, two-colour risograph, a blue-graded photo). Picked: **flat
  gouache** in Graphite's slate greys and charcoal with a few cobalt touches. Its mid-tone backgrounds sit on both
  looks, it still reads at phone size, and it has no monsters. The same cast and the same card moments were redrawn
  (`scripts/art/base-story-art.py`), and the set is bundled in the app. The 9 AM studio still draws new stories in
  clay; switching it to gouache comes with book downloads (B2), and needs a yes because it publishes.

## Eat: the food log

- One box: type what you ate ("2 eggs, 180 g rice, a banana"), say it, or take a photo. You choose the detail.
- **Free:** add a food by hand (name, amount, calories, protein), and re-add recent foods in one tap.
- **Plus:** AI turns the sentence, the voice note or the photo into foods with grams, calories, protein, carbs and fat.
  Nothing saves until you check it, and every number can be fixed.
- Voice starts with the keyboard's own microphone (works today, costs nothing); a dedicated mic button can come later.
- Daily targets for calories and protein from the goal (lose, keep, gain) and body weight, in line with the Kaizen
  bulk/cut planner.

## How it's built

- **Same app, new screens.** The game screens stay in the code, unreachable, and are removed in a later clean-up once
  the base app is settled. The game's tables and functions on the server stay untouched, so 0.14.x phones keep
  working until they update.
- **New tables** (migrations from 0023, RLS on every one): training plans, workouts, sets, kept swaps; books and
  chapter order on stories, reading progress; food entries and food targets.
- **New server functions:** one for the base app's saving and state (plan, history, library, today's food), and one
  for AI food logging that follows the onboarding call's pattern (spend reserved first, JSON answer checked, real cost
  recorded).
- **The rule change:** CLAUDE.md's "server-authoritative progression" was about XP. With no XP, the server keeps the
  record and the shared rules package works out the plan's week the same way on the phone and the server.
- **Look:** the owner turned down Mint Night (2026-10-28). Ten looks are proposed on
  https://claude.ai/artifact/MruxJc4VBCFRw439bnjn1R (dark: Graphite, Volt, Navy and Coral, Plum, Midnight Mono;
  light: Library, Three Colors, Poster, Raspberry, Android Dynamic), each on Today and the workout log, every text
  pair checked at 4.5:1 or better. The picked look gets its light or dark partner, and its font is bundled like M PLUS
  Rounded 1c is today (a font download, so it waits for the owner's yes). Lilita One goes with the game.
- **Testers:** their past workouts (reps only, as that's all we kept) and the stories they read are copied into the
  new tables.
