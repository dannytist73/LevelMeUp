Why tiny wins change how you see yourself

Most people try to change what they do. The stubborn ones change who they think they are, and the doing follows.

Every rep is a vote

Each time you keep a small promise, you collect evidence about yourself. One push-up proves very little. Thirty days of them make a case your own mind finds hard to argue with.[1]

Small beats heroic

Big efforts depend on mood. Small ones survive bad days, and the days you show up anyway are the ones that rewrite your story. Studies of habit suggest that repeating something in the same setting matters more than how hard you go.[2]
