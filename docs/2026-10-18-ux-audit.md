# UX audit, 2026-10-18 (Lantern build, M11)

**In plain words.** The app is sound underneath. Text contrast passes everywhere, and every animation has a
reduced-motion path. The two daily jobs, doing a quest and reading, have hierarchy and flow problems:

- Today shows several accent buttons at once.
- A quest's log button sits about ten blocks down its screen.
- Most tappable things give no press feedback.
- The daily loop takes 9 to 14 taps across two separate "Cleared" screens.

The proposed redesign is the "Round 3 · UI/UX Pro Max" page of the proposals canvas
(https://claude.ai/artifact/Tfm6zj7rAxH6wFyEsqDtBF).

**Method.** A read-only code audit (a subagent) against the UI/UX Pro Max skill's priorities: accessibility, touch,
hierarchy, navigation, feedback, onboarding, consistency and motion. Contrast was computed from `src/theme/tokens.ts`.
Anything that depends on device rendering is marked unverified. Paths are under `apps/mobile/`.

## The daily loop today

**Reps, workout, timer or sentence core quest:**
1. Tap the card, then scroll past about ten blocks to the log.
2. Log, then Continue on the Cleared screen.
3. Back on Today, find the reading and tap Read.
4. 3 to 7 card turns, the takeaway field, Claim, Continue.

That comes to 9 to 14 taps and 6 screen changes (Today → Quest → Cleared → Today → Deck → Cleared → Today).

**Extra stops that can be added:**
- The Shadow scene opening by itself.
- A check-in.
- Each notice's "Got it".
- Level-up and achievement moments.

## Biggest redesign opportunities

1. **Today as a "next action" screen.** One "Up next" block holds the only accent button. Done work collapses, and the
   day closes once the core quest and the reading are done. *That last part is a game rule: "Day N cleared" currently
   needs the side quests too.*
2. **The quest flow built around the action.** A sticky log bar, and Cleared leading on to the next quest or the
   reading.
3. **Reading as a first-class step with a home.** A Library tab with today's reading, kept lessons that open their
   deck, and past readings.
4. **One information architecture and one screen frame.** Today / Library / Character / Chronicle, the gear on every
   tab, and one top bar with Back.
5. **An onboarding cut to what Day 1 needs.** Name, goals, safety and rhythm, then the reveal. Voice, gear, the slip
   choice, the vow, the Pact and Google save move to days 1 to 3. Aim for under 90 seconds to the first quest; today it
   is roughly 2 to 3 minutes (an estimate).
