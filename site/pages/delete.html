Delete your LevelMeUp account and data

You can delete your character and everything kept with it at any time, with or without the app.

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open LevelMeUp and tap the gear on Today to open Settings.
  2. Scroll to the end and tap Delete my character.
  3. Read what goes, then confirm. It happens at once.

Without the app

Email __CONTACT_EMAIL__ from the Google account your character is saved with, with the subject "Delete my LevelMeUp account". We delete it within 30 days and reply when it's done. A guest character that was never saved with Google can only be deleted from the app, because nothing links it to you.

What is deleted

  • Your character: name, class, stats, levels, streaks and achievements.
  • Your answers, quests, logs, check-ins, notes and reading takeaways.
  • Your LevelMeUp Plus record, and your purchase history at RevenueCat.
  • A guest sign-in, and everything the app kept on your phone.

What is kept

  • A Google sign-in record (email, name and picture link) stays unless you ask us to remove it too: say so in your email and we delete it as well.
  • Google keeps its own records of Google Play purchases, as payment law requires.
  • A count of AI use, with nothing that identifies you, stays for our cost records.

Deleting does not cancel a Plus subscription. Cancel it first in the Google Play app: tap your profile picture, then Payments and subscriptions → Subscriptions → LevelMeUp Plus → Cancel.
