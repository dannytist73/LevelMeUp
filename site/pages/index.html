LevelMeUp

Real-life quests, one day at a time

LevelMeUp is an Android app that turns small daily habits into quests. You get a class, five stats that grow as you train, levels, a short reading each day, and rest days and a pain route when you need them.

The whole game is free. LevelMeUp Plus, an optional subscription, adds two more colour themes, charts of your stats over all time and portrait frames.
