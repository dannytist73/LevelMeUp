Privacy policy

Last updated __UPDATED__. This policy covers the LevelMeUp app for Android and this website.

LevelMeUp ("we", "us") is a self-improvement game: daily real-life quests, a class, stats and levels. This page says what we keep, where it is kept, who can see it, and how to delete it. We don't sell your data, show ads or track you across other apps.

What we keep on our server

  • Your character: the name you choose, your class, stats, level, streaks, achievements and the short "reveal" text written for you.
  • The answers that shape your quests: your goals, training setup and equipment, minutes per day, difficulty, voice, any physical limits you tell us about, your time zone and when your day ends, books you name, and your accountability pact level.
  • Your days: the quests you were given and what you logged (for example sets, reps and timer minutes), your check-ins and their answers, private notes you write, takeaways from readings, and which readings you were given.
  • Your sign-in: a guest account, or, if you save your character with Google, the email address, name and profile picture link Google shares with the sign-in. The app shows the email and uses none of the rest.
  • LevelMeUp Plus: whether you are on Plus, which plan, and until when.
  • Technical logs: like any server, ours keeps short logs of requests, such as your IP address, for about a week.

What stays only on your phone

Your vow, your portrait and look choices, your notification times, the lessons you keep in your Codex, readings saved for days without a connection, and anything not yet sent.

What we never keep

Your answers to the safety questions at the start (only whether a gentle start was recommended is kept). We don't collect your location, contacts, photos, advertising ID or data from other apps.

How we use it

Only to run the game for you: to build each day's quests, count your progress, show your history, send the reminders you turn on, and keep your character safe if you change phones. The developer can see the database and looks only at totals, such as how many players chose each class, and at progress without names; those reports never show names or notes.

Who handles it for us

  • Supabase stores the data above, in Singapore. Only your own sign-in can read your data from the app.
  • Anthropic (Claude, an AI model) writes your reveal. For that, your character's name, voice, class, starting stats, goals, minutes, difficulty, any limits and whether you start gently are sent to Claude: once, and again if you change your class or an answer on the reveal, at most three times. The daily readings are written ahead for every player and use nothing about you.
  • Google runs the optional sign-in and takes payments for Plus through Google Play. We never see your card details.
  • RevenueCat checks Google Play's receipts for us and keeps your purchase history under your player number, never your name or email.

These companies handle data only to provide their service to us. We share nothing with anyone else, except where the law requires it.

Security

Everything travels encrypted (HTTPS). On the server, each player can read only their own rows. Your sign-in on the phone is stored encrypted.

How long we keep it

As long as your character exists. When you delete it, we delete everything listed above (see below). Technical logs expire after about a week. Google keeps its own records of Play purchases, as payment law requires.

Deleting your data

In the app: Settings → Delete my character. This removes your character and everything kept with it, on our server, at RevenueCat and on your phone. A guest sign-in is deleted with it. A Google sign-in record stays (your email, name and picture link) unless you ask us to remove it. Deleting does not cancel a Plus subscription: cancel that in Google Play. Without the app, see Delete your account and data.

Not medical advice

LevelMeUp is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any condition. Talk to a health professional before starting a new exercise routine, and stop if anything hurts.

Age

LevelMeUp is made for adults, 18 and over.

Changes

If this policy changes, we update this page and its date. Big changes are also shown in the app.
